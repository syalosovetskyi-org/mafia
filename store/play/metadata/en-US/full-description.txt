One phone for the entire table: this app handles all the duties of a moderator for the classic party game Mafia (Werewolf). It deals secret roles, tracks who is who, guides night and day phases with a clear script, resolves night actions, keeps speech timers, tracks player fouls, and plays background music for each phase.

Fully offline, no advertisements, no accounts or registrations, and zero runtime permissions required. Lightweight download (~2 MB).

KEY FEATURES:

1. Flexible Table Setup
• From 4 to 20 players.
• Automatic balance calculation (≈ one-third black team) with manual adjustment for easier or tougher games.
• Optional roles: Don (Godfather), Sheriff (Detective), Doctor, Butterfly (Escort), Maniac (Serial Killer).
• "First night in the air" mode (safe first night) for balanced red/black games.
• Adjustable speech durations and intro round timing.

2. Secret Role Dealing
• Pass the phone around the table.
• Players press and hold the card to reveal their role and portrait. Release to immediately hide it — no accidental role leaks to the next player.
• Screenshot and screen-recording protection (FLAG_SECURE) on secret role screens.

3. Step-by-Step Night Phase
• Strict order of actions: Butterfly → Mafia → Don → Doctor → Sheriff → Maniac.
• Only living eligible players appear on screen at each step.
• Instant moderator hints: faction alignments (red/black) are shown during Sheriff's turn; the Sheriff is marked during Don's search.

4. Automatic Morning Resolution
• Night results are calculated automatically: Butterfly blocks target's action, Doctor saves the victim, Mafia and Maniac shots apply independently.
• The morning screen clearly lists all casualties and their roles.

5. Day Phase, Speeches & Timer
• Clear queue of speakers for each round.
• Interactive countdown timer: start, pause, reset, and an audio alert when time runs out.
• Nominate players for voting directly during speeches.

6. Foul System
• 1 foul — verbal warning.
• 2 fouls — skip the next speech round (one-time penalty).
• 3 fouls — immediate disqualification and elimination from the game.
• Easily add or remove fouls at any day phase, including introduction.

7. Voting & Win Conditions
• Vote on nominated candidates.
• Vote counting, runoff votes, and moderator tie-breakers.
• Automatic win detection for Town, Mafia, or Maniac.

8. Cheatsheet & Event Log
• One-tap cheatsheet: full overview of all players, their roles, and alive/eliminated status.
• Event log tracks everything that happened during the game.
• Quick exit back to setup at any time.

9. Atmosphere & Soundtrack
• Built-in procedural noir soundtrack: ambient loops for night, day, voting, plus sound effects (gong, gunshot, morning chime, timer alert).
• Custom track support: pick your own music files or folders for each phase using the system file picker — without granting storage permissions.
• Floating mini-player at the bottom of the screen to control playback.
